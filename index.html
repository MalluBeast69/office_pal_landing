About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at Calicut University, passionate about building practical, real-world applications and continuously growing as a developer. I specialize in Flutter app development, with a strong focus on creating clean, minimalistic UIs and optimizing user experience.

One of my standout projects is a university-level exam and seating arrangement system that handles complex logic such as dynamic seat allocation, faculty assignments, and student authentication—designed with real-world scalability in mind. I actively use design and development tools such as Figma for UI/UX, Supabase and Firebase for backend services, and version control with GitHub.

I also incorporate AI tools like Cursor AI and ChatGPT to boost coding productivity and accuracy. As a self-taught learner, I stay up-to-date with the latest developments in AI, regularly exploring innovations such as Google Gemini 1.5 Pro, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, DeepSeek-V2, and other cutting-edge models.

I've also built smaller personal apps to solve day-to-day problems—like a volume control utility—demonstrating my problem-solving mindset. I value clean code, thoughtful design, and continuous learning as key drivers of long-term success.
